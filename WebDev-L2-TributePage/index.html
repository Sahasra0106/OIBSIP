HIS STORY

About John McCarthy

John McCarthy was an American computer scientist and one of the founding pioneers of Artificial Intelligence. He introduced the term "Artificial Intelligence" in 1955 and played a major role in establishing AI as a field of academic research.

His work in computer programming, logical reasoning and intelligent machines influenced generations of researchers and helped shape modern computing.

HIS ACHIEVEMENTS

Major Contributions

🤖

Artificial Intelligence

Introduced the term Artificial Intelligence in the 1955 proposal for the Dartmouth Summer Research Project.

💻

LISP Programming

Created LISP in 1958, a programming language that became highly influential in AI research.

🧠

Time-Sharing Systems

Helped pioneer time-sharing systems, allowing multiple users to interact with a computer.
